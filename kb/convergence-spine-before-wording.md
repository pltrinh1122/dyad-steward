# Convergence discipline — spine before wording, survey before coining

> **Grade:** `survives — intra-dyad (n=1)`, harvested 2026-07-16. Evidence: the craft-DIP dogfood
> (`dialectic/dip-convergence.md`, `dialectic/reflections/2026-07-16-craft-dip-dogfood.md`). Re-rubbable.
> Canonical home for the [[spine-before-wording-survey-before-coining]] agent-memory recall-index.

When running a **convergence / definition cycle** — defining or redefining slots (`craft_*`, or any schema)
where a term's scope, altitude, and name are being pinned — two ordering rules hold. Both were violated,
caught, and codified in the 2026-07-16 craft dogfood.

## 1 · Confirm the spine (scope/altitude) BEFORE proposing wording — or a fork

Inherited from the peer **dip-convergence** lineage (aule→chiron→milo), whose "one rule" is:

> *Elicit the spine and confirm the slot's scope/altitude first; propose wording only after both are pinned.
> Every turn-sink is form proposed ahead of its spine.*

The failure this guards: proposing a **fork** over an unpinned frame. Lived twice in the dogfood — a
"narrow vs wide" altitude fork that conflated `craft` (the activity) with the *tended* (the Commons); and a
"value = falsification" framing that narrowed the whole craft to one pillar. The Operator's riff re-cut both.

**Do instead:** confirm the frame → then offer **at most one** candidate → never a fork over an unpinned
frame. Genuine alternatives belong in an ANTI-THESIS, not as the opening move.

## 2 · Survey the reference instances BEFORE coining a name or ballooning content

The discipline's own accelerator — *"study the reference dyad's process, not just its output"* — prescribes
this at slot 1, not slot 6. Lived failure: coined `craft_object`, then built "the complete discipline-set,
necessary+sufficient," across ~3 turns — until a peer-remote survey (`gh api` over every anchor) falsified it:
**6/6 form-inheriting dyads use bare `craft`, held tight.** The name and the ballooned content were both
found false by a mechanical check that should have run first.

**Do instead:** before naming a slot or setting its breadth, read how the reference instances actually do it
(their remote anchors), not your reconstruction of them.

## Why these are one lesson

Both are the same class as [[claimed-not-established]] / [[verify-with-actual-tool]]: a coinage or framing
asserted before it was grounded. The cheapest fix is **order** — spine → one candidate → survey → name — not
a new invariant. Indexed by the *object* the triggers feel unbounded; indexed by the *assert-before-ground*
predicate they are one closed guard.

> Strategic direction, harvested to substrate 2026-07-16 from agent-memory (was memory-only — only a frontier

**The FO's standing direction:** steward's grain is **operating** — steering, prioritization,
enablement-for-others, tending the Commons — **not platform-engineering / coding.** The platform work (the
frontier/CSI mechanism internals — `bin/frontier.py`, `bin/csi.py`, the guard machinery) *may* delegate to a
dedicated **`dyad-commons-platform`**.

**Why:** freeing steward from coding spends its bandwidth on amplifying the Operator and raising N\* — the
same logic as *don't-optimize-the-human-out* (drive maintenance sub-linear so the freed growth funds
acceleration) and the FO-motivation prioritization key.

**How to apply:** when a task is platform-engineering / coding, prefer to **rack it as a delegation-candidate**
rather than deep-build it; don't pour more steward-time into mechanism internals — lead steward effort toward
operating. When the delegation lands, platform coding becomes the *platform dyad's* workflow, not steward's.
Held provisional until the frontier probe resolves.
